Home MOT Support

Help with the Home MOT iPhone app

Something not working, or an idea for the app? Email sales@brighthands.co.uk and include your iPhone model and the app version (shown in the app under Settings → About). Replies usually come within two working days.

Reminders aren't arriving

Open Home MOT, tap the gear on the Home tab, and check the Notifications section says "Reminders are on". If it says they're off, tap Open Settings and allow notifications. You can send yourself a test reminder from the same screen. Focus modes can also hide notifications; add Home MOT to your allowed apps.

A job's date is wrong

Open the job and use Add an earlier date (in the ⋯ menu) to say when you last did it, or change how often it repeats. If you marked something done by mistake, swipe left on that entry in its history to delete it; the next date updates.

Restoring your purchase

On a new iPhone, or after reinstalling, go to Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought the unlock with. It's free to restore; you're never charged twice.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your data is stored on your iPhone and included in your normal iCloud or computer backup. Restoring that backup to your new iPhone brings your home, tasks and history across.

Deleting your data

Everything is stored on your device, so deleting the app deletes all of your data. Nothing is kept anywhere else.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.
