Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Short version: KeepWell Home does not collect any of your data. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

Your home details, tasks, completion history, notes, photos and renewal dates are saved only on your device, inside the app. The developer cannot see them.

iCloud

If you turn on iCloud sync, your data is stored in your own private iCloud account so it can sync between your devices. It is protected by Apple and is not accessible to the developer. You can turn sync off at any time.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server to deliver them.

Photos

If you add a photo to a completed job, the app only reads the photo you pick. It has no access to the rest of your photo library.

Purchases

The one-off unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store. The app only learns whether the unlock has been bought. It never sees your payment details.

Children

The app is not aimed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
