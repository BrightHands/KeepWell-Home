What it does

Everything your home needs, in one place.

Know what to do, and why.

Every job explains why it matters and how to do it, with links to official UK guidance from Gas Safe, the HSE, fire services and more.

  • Safety jobs come first
  • Snooze for a day, a week or a month
  • A reminder on the day, or one summary every Sunday

A service history for your home.

Mark a job done with a date, a note and a photo of the receipt or certificate. Handy when you sell, let or remortgage.

  • Already done something? Add when, and the next date moves to match
  • Notes and photos stay with each job

Life admin, sorted.

MOT, car and home insurance, passport, TV licence, broadband and energy contracts. Get a nudge before each one ends, so you can switch rather than auto-renew.

  • Reminders weeks ahead, when quotes are cheapest
  • Built-in CP12 and EICR reminders for landlords
